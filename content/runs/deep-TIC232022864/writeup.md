A follow-up on the one signal from sector 88 that passed the automatic checks.

The first search used BLS, which fits a box-shaped dip. This time Kepler ran Transit Least Squares on TIC 232022864. TLS fits a realistic rounded transit shape and does better on very shallow signals. If the 7.09-day, 49 ppm dip were real, TLS should have found it at least as clearly.

It didn't. The strongest period TLS found was 0.61 days, with a detection score (SDE) of 7.9, below the threshold for a real signal. Nothing near 7.09 days.

That's the end of the line for this one. A signal that only appears with one method and vanishes with a better one is almost always noise. TIC 232022864 stays marked *probably false positive*. The artwork shows the ghost it turned out to be.
